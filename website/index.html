/ features

Everything you'd want, nothing you didn't ask for

No sign-up, no server, no telemetry. AirClipboard is a WebSocket handshake between two devices on the same network — that's the whole product.

Zero cloud

Clipboard data never leaves your Wi-Fi network. There is no server to trust — because there is no server.

AES-256 encrypted

Every pairing generates a unique shared key. Content is encrypted end-to-end with AES-GCM, always.

Instant

Copy and it's already on the other device. A live WebSocket connection, not a polling sync job.

Full history

Browse and re-copy anything you've synced recently — stored locally, cleared whenever you want.

/ how it works

Paired in under a minute

01

Open both apps

AirClipboard sits quietly in your Mac menu bar and advertises itself over mDNS — Android finds it automatically.

02

Scan or confirm

Scan the QR code on Mac, or connect manually by IP. Accept the one-time pairing code and you're trusted.

03

Copy anywhere

From then on, every copy on either device shows up on the other — automatically, silently, instantly.
